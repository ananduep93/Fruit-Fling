Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

Welcome to Fruit Fling! Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in our HTML5 game.

1. Information We Collect

Fruit Fling is a fully client-side HTML5 game. We do not run any backend servers, nor do we collect, store, or transmit any personal data, account credentials, device identifiers, or usage statistics. Your privacy is fully preserved.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

The game uses browser local storage (specifically localStorage) to save your level completion status, star records, coins, and shop cosmetics. This data stays entirely on your local device and is never uploaded to any remote server. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Third-Party Services

If you play Fruit Fling on publishing platforms (such as CrazyGames), those platforms may collect anonymous analytics, present advertisements, or store your game data using their own accounts or systems. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on how they process your information.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the game does not collect any personal data, it is fully safe for players of all ages, including children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
