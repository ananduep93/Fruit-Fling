💡 How to Play

Drag the fruit backwards on the slingshot and release to launch it!

🔥 Special Abilities: Click/tap anywhere on the screen while the fruit is in the air to activate its power!

🍎 Apple: Apple Smash (slams downwards)
🍌 Banana: Banana Boost (extra speed & lift)
🥥 Coconut: Coco Bomb (explodes mid-air/impact)
🍓 Strawberry: Grow (becomes giant & heavy)
🍉 Watermelon: Melon Split (splits into 3)
